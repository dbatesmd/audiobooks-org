Dale, Fry, or full-cast? The three narrations compared

Same books, three very different listens — this is the real choice in this series. Ratings are Audible's for Sorcerer's Stone, checked November 4, 2026; every edition of every book sits between 4.8 and 4.9, so the differences are taste, price and where you can hear them — not quality.

Jim Dale — the American recording

One narrator performing every character — for two decades the default US listen, and still the most-rated: ★ 4.9 from 204,276 ratings on Audible (Book 1). Book 1 runs 8 hrs 18; the set totals about 116 hours.

Cost: Kobo $29.99 (Books 1–3) and $44.99 (Books 4–7) — $269.93 for all seven — or one Audible credit per book. Included: all seven on Kobo Plus Listen ($7.99/mo) and on Spotify Premium in North America. Free: Hoopla, and Libby/OverDrive catalogues (expect holds).

Stephen Fry — the British recording

A warmer, storyteller's reading — less a one-man cast, more being read to by the ideal uncle. ★ 4.9 from 3,486 ratings on Audible US (Book 1): the same score as Dale from a smaller pool, because the US listing is newer. Book 1 runs 8 hrs 25.

Cost: mirrors Dale on Kobo US where checked ($29.99 for Book 1, $44.99 for Goblet of Fire), or one Audible credit per book. Included: Spotify Premium in every market — and in the US both narrations are on Spotify, so you can pick your voice; Kobo's Fry listings are also in Kobo Plus. Free: listed on Hoopla.

Full-cast — the Audible Original dramatisation (2025–26)

Not a reading but a production: 200+ actors across the series, sound design, Dolby Atmos. ★ 4.8 from 17,635 ratings on Book 1 — a notch behind the single narrators there, but level with Dale at 4.9 by Goblet of Fire. Book 1 runs 8 hrs 41; the set was completed in May 2026.

Cost and availability: Audible only — one credit per book. It is not sold by any other store and is not on Spotify, Kobo Plus, Hoopla or Libby; there is no subscription or free route to this version.
