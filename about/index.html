About Audiobooks.org

Audiobooks.org is an independent guide to listening. We track the newest audiobooks, check what each one costs store by store, find the free route when there is one, and label every narrator — human or AI — so you can decide how you want to listen.

Three ways to listen

Almost every audiobook can be had one of three ways. Free — with a library card (Libby, Hoopla, Kanopy) or from free sources like LibriVox and the Internet Archive; the trade-off is waitlists and borrow limits. Cheap — buy the book outright on a deal, keep it forever, no subscription; the trade-off is hunting for the price. Unlimited — a membership or all-you-can-listen plan; the trade-off is that you stop owning anything when you stop paying. This site is organised around those three choices, and every book page shows whichever of them actually apply.

How we check prices

Every price on this site was read off the store's own page and carries the date it was checked. If we haven't verified a figure, we don't print one — the slot stays blank or marked Pending rather than showing a guess. Audible is listed as "1 credit / check price" because what you pay there depends on your membership; we won't invent a cash price. When a book is free somewhere we've verified — a library app, LibriVox, the Internet Archive — the free route is always shown first. And if a price gets too old to trust, we hide the number rather than show a stale one.

How we label narration

Each book page names the narrator of the edition it describes, and the site-wide Human / Both / AI switch filters on that label. A label comes from the publisher's and stores' credits for that edition — one retailer's metadata flag on its own is never enough for us to call a human performance AI, or the reverse. On the AI in audiobooks page you can hear dated side-by-side samples and judge the current state of AI narration for yourself; the date matters, because what you hear now is the worst it will ever be.

Privacy: what we log

There are no accounts on Audiobooks.org. When you use the search box, we log the search anonymously and in aggregate — which titles people look for decides which book pages we build next. We don't store IP addresses with searches, and we don't sell data. Like most sites we use Google Analytics to count visits.

How the site is supported

Audiobooks.org is reader-supported: we may earn a commission from Audible, Libro.fm, or Chirp links. A commission never changes which option we recommend first, and the free route is always shown whether it pays us or not — most free routes pay nothing.
