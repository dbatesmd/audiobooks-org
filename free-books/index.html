Free first. Always.

Great audiobooks, $0. Start with your library card.

The best price in audiobooks is free — and it's legal, instant to set up, and bigger than most people realise. Libby and Hoopla lend you the same audiobooks the stores sell. The one honest trade-off: popular titles can have a hold queue. We'll show you how to work it.

Start here: free in 10 minutes

Step 1
Get a library card

Your local library card is the key. No card? Many libraries issue a digital card online in minutes.

Step 2
Install Libby (and Hoopla)

Libby is the main library app; Hoopla is the no-waitlist one — if your library offers it, some big releases are free from day one.

Step 3
Place holds early, listen free

New releases get queues. Our new-release pages flag the free route and the wait, so you can decide: wait free, or grab a cheap copy instead.

The free sources directory

Every source below is legal and free — no trial, no credit card. Ported from our free-sources directory; per-source counts are each source's own published figures.

No account needed

Open and listen instantly — zero sign-up required

17,000+ free audiobooks · Public domain
LibriVox

Volunteer-recorded public domain audiobooks. Every title is in the public domain and read by real volunteers from around the world. No account, no DRM, no fees — ever. Download MP3s directly or stream in-browser.

Visit librivox.org →

6,000+ audio titles · No DRM downloads
Project Gutenberg Audio

Classic literature in audio form — many read by human volunteers, some generated with permission. Free to download and keep forever, no DRM. The same beloved source as the ebook collection, now with audio.

Visit gutenberg.org →

7,000+ titles · Podcast-feed compatible
Loyal Books

A clean, well-organized directory of free public domain audiobooks sourced from LibriVox and other archives. Easy to browse by genre, author, or top-rated picks. Podcast-feed integration lets you follow books in any podcast app.

Visit loyalbooks.com →

1,000+ curated links · Celebrity narrators included
Open Culture

A curated collection of the best free audiobooks from around the web — including celebrity-narrated titles, BBC recordings, and rare public domain gems. Not a hosting site, but an exceptional discovery hub linking to the best free content.

Visit openculture.com →

Free with a library card

Your public library card unlocks thousands of audiobooks for free

Millions of titles via your local library · Bestsellers included
Libby / OverDrive

The gold standard of library audiobooks. Sign in with your library card and borrow from a catalog of millions of titles — including recent bestsellers. Loans are typically 14–21 days. Works on iOS, Android, Kindle, and desktop; OverDrive powers the backend for libraries worldwide.

Visit libbyapp.com →

Thousands of titles · No waitlists · Instant access
Hoopla Digital

Instant, wait-free borrowing via your library card — no holds queue. While Libby titles sometimes have waiting lists, Hoopla is always immediately available. Includes audiobooks, ebooks, comics, movies, and music. Monthly borrow limit varies by library (typically 8–15 titles).

Visit hoopladigital.com →

Select libraries · Audiobooks + video streaming
Kanopy

Primarily a video streaming service via libraries, but Kanopy also offers audiobooks through select library partnerships. Best known for indie films and documentaries — check if your library participates, as coverage varies by system.

Visit kanopy.com →

Thousands of titles · Growing library network
cloudLibrary

A growing library borrowing platform similar to Libby. Many library systems use cloudLibrary as an alternative or supplement to OverDrive. Clean app, solid catalog — worth checking if your library participates, and it often has shorter wait times for popular titles.

Visit yourcloudlibrary.com →

Podcast-style & serialized

Full books distributed as free podcast feeds

Indie titles · Free podcast feeds · Pay-what-you-want
Scribl (formerly Podiobooks)

Indie audiobooks released as free podcast feeds — subscribe in any podcast app and listen chapter by chapter, as the episodes drop or all at once. Formerly Podiobooks; authors set the price, and much of the catalogue is free or pay-what-you-want.

Visit scribl.com →

100,000+ recordings · No account needed
Internet Archive — Audio Books & Poetry

The Internet Archive's own audiobook and poetry collection — a deep archive of recordings to stream or download with no account and no fees. Home to volunteer readings and the Project Gutenberg Open Audiobook Collection's AI-narrated classics.

Visit archive.org/details/audio_bookspoetry →

💡 Pro tip: The fastest path to free bestsellers is your library card + Libby. Most public library cards are free to sign up for — check your local library's website. If your library doesn't participate in Libby, try Hoopla as a no-waitlist alternative.
