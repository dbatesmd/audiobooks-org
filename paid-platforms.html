💛 Paid platforms

Find your perfect audiobook subscription

Side-by-side comparison of every major paid platform — pricing, catalog size, and what makes each one worth it.

ℹ️ Affiliate disclosure: Some links on this page earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps keep Audiobooks.org free.

All paid platforms

7 platforms compared · Updated May 2026

📚

Audible by Amazon

The world's largest audiobook catalog with over 750,000 titles. Members get 1 credit per month to spend on any title, plus access to Audible Plus — a rotating library of thousands of included titles. You keep every book you buy, even if you cancel.

30-day free trial $14.95/mo after trial 750,000+ titles
Try free 30 days ↗
📖

Scribd

Unlimited streaming access to audiobooks, ebooks, sheet music, magazines, and documents — all for one flat monthly price. Best value if you're a heavy reader who wants variety. Catalog is curated but wide-ranging.

Unlimited access $11.99/mo 1M+ titles (audiobooks + ebooks)
Get unlimited ↗
🏠

Libro.fm

Same catalog as Audible, but every purchase directly supports your chosen independent bookshop. Great if you want to listen guilt-free — your $14.99/mo credit goes to a local store instead of Amazon.

Supports indie stores $14.99/mo 400,000+ titles
Browse Libro.fm ↗
🌍

Storytel

Europe's leading audiobook platform now available in the US and globally. Unlimited streaming with a strong international catalog and exclusive Storytel Originals. Particularly good for non-English titles and Scandinavian literature.

14-day free trial $14.99/mo 500,000+ titles
Try Storytel ↗
⬇️

Downpour

Own-your-audio platform from Blackstone Publishing. DRM-free MP3 downloads mean your books play on any device, forever. Club membership gives you a monthly credit plus 30% off à-la-carte purchases. A great Audible alternative for ownership purists.

DRM-free downloads $12.99/mo (club) 100,000+ titles
Try Downpour ↗
▶️

Google Play Books

No subscription required — buy individual audiobooks à-la-carte and they're yours forever, synced across all your Google-connected devices. Great for casual listeners who don't want a monthly commitment. Frequent sales on popular titles.

No subscription needed Pay-per-title Large catalog
Browse Google Play ↗
🍎

Apple Books

Apple's built-in bookstore for iPhone and iPad users. Buy and own individual titles — no subscription needed. Seamlessly integrated with iOS and macOS. Strong catalog of bestsellers with frequent deals and curated staff picks.

No subscription needed Pay-per-title Apple devices only
Browse Apple Books ↗
