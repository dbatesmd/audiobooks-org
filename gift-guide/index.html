Holiday Gift Guide · 2026

Give listening

An audiobook is the rare gift that fits in an email, arrives instantly, and lasts twenty hours. Here's how to give one well — a membership, a single great book, or a card — with the catches spelled out before you pay.

Give a membership

Best when you don't know exactly what they'd pick. None of these auto-renews on the recipient.

Audible gift membership

$15 · $45 · $90 · $150

1, 3, 6 or 12 months, one credit a month plus the Plus Catalog. If they already belong, they simply get all the credits at once. Catch: gift credits expire 12 months after they're issued, and there's no standalone Audible gift card anymore — this membership is the gift card.

Give an Audible gift membership →

Libro.fm credit bundles

2–24 credits · $14.99 a credit

The thoughtful alternative: credits that never expire, DRM-free books the listener actually owns, and a share of your purchase goes to an independent bookstore you choose. Arrives by email — now, scheduled, or as a printable certificate.

Give Libro.fm credits →

Spotify gift card

$12 · $36 · $72 · $120

Premium includes 15 hours of audiobooks a month, so this doubles as a listening gift. One catch worth knowing: the cards now top up a balance, not a set number of months — Premium is $12.99/mo, so a $12 card covers about 28 days, not a full month.

The free gift

A library card costs nothing, takes ten minutes to get, and unlocks Libby and Hoopla — thousands of audiobooks, free, legally. Pair the card with two or three of our free-route picks and you've given a year of listening for $0.

Before you send

  • Expiry: Audible gift credits die after 12 months; Libro.fm credits and Chirp gifts never expire.
  • Region: Chirp gifts work only in the US & Canada; Spotify cards only redeem in the country they were bought in.
  • Timing: every option above can be scheduled to land on the day — or printed and handed over.
  • Already a member? An Audible gift still lands as credits; a Libro.fm bundle lands the same way. Nothing is wasted.
