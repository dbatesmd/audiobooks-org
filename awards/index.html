Awards

Audiobook awards

The honours that matter in listening — who won, who narrated, and the cheapest way to hear each winner. Listed newest first, and kept current through the awards year.

The awards year

When each lands

January — Odyssey Award, Voice Arts Awards · February — the Grammy · Late winter — Audie finalists · March — Audie winners, London Book Fair excellence awards · May — British Book Awards · November — Grammy nominations, the Speakies (British Audio Awards) · December — year-end best-of lists · Each month — AudioFile Earphones picks, announced at the month's start.

November 23, 2026 — The Speakies

The British Audio Awards · winners still to come

Britain's dedicated audiobook awards, run by The Bookseller and The Stage — only in their second year, and already up to 423 entries. The shortlists are out; the winners are named at the Queen Elizabeth Hall, Southbank, on November 23, hosted by Sally Phillips. The category everyone watches is Narrator:

2026 · Narrator shortlist
Adjoa Andoh (Cleopatra) · Joe Eyre (Assassin's Apprentice) · Hanako Footman (The Rainshadow Orphans) · Rory Kinnear (Jesus Christ Kinski) · James McAvoy (Recall — his first audiobook) · Simon Russell Beale (the George Smiley novels)

Shortlists via Voice Over Herald, from The Bookseller's published lists. This section gets the winners on November 23.

November 2026 — AudioFile Earphones Awards

Announced at the start of each month

AudioFile Magazine's Earphones Awards mark exceptional narration — the steadiest flow of new honourees in listening, announced as a fresh round at the start of every month. November's round is the current one; this section refreshes as each new round lands, and AudioFile's Best of the Year list follows in December.

May 11, 2026 — The British Book Awards

The "Nibbies" · London

The UK book trade's big night, at Grosvenor House — with audiobooks honoured in fiction and nonfiction:

2026 · Audiobook of the Year — Fiction
Cursed Daughters — Oyinkan Braithwaite · Narrated by Weruche Opia, Diana Yekinni and Nnei Opia Clark · WF Howes
2026 · Audiobook of the Year — Non-Fiction
Careless People — Sarah Wynn-Williams · Written and narrated by the author · Macmillan

Full winners list at Listening Books.

March 2026 — London Book Fair Excellence Awards

Presented at the fair, March 10–12

The London Book Fair's International Excellence Awards name an Audiobook Publisher of the Year — Penguin Random House Audio took it this year, a useful signal for where the production money is going.

March 2, 2026 — The Audie Awards

Announced each March

The Audio Publishers Association's awards — the industry's Oscars, with 25+ categories across fiction, nonfiction, narration and audio drama, plus the big one:

2026 · Audiobook of the Year
Sunrise on the Reaping — Suzanne Collins · Narrated by Jefferson White · Scholastic Audio (announced March 2, 2026)
2026 · Performance awards
Best Fiction Narrator: Kristin Atherton (Outlander) · Best Nonfiction Narrator: Blair Underwood (Truly) · Best Ensemble: The Correspondent
Recent Audiobook of the Year winners
2025: My Name Is Barbra — Barbra Streisand, narrated by the author · 2023: Finding Me — Viola Davis, narrated by the author

Full category list at audiopub.org. Winners with a price-check on this site link straight to it; the rest join the checking queue.

February 1, 2026 — The Grammy

Nominations each November · winner February

One category — Best Audio Book, Narration & Storytelling Recording — but the one that makes the general news, especially when a famous voice wins.

2026 winner
Meditations: The Reflections of His Holiness the Dalai Lama — the Dalai Lama's first Grammy, at the 68th awards on February 1, 2026. The shortlist also carried Ketanji Brown Jackson and Trevor Noah.

Recent winners run from Viola Davis (2023 — the win that completed her EGOT) to Michelle Obama (2024). The 2027 nominees are announced November 16, 2026 — this section updates then.

January 26, 2026 — The Odyssey Award

ALA Youth Media Awards

The American Library Association's award for the best audiobook production for children and young adults, announced with the Newbery and Caldecott. The award goes to the producer — it's a production honour, which is why Odyssey winners so often sound extraordinary.

2026 · Children
Clack, Clack! Smack! A Cherokee Stickball Story — Traci Sorell · Produced by Live Oak Media · Narrated by the author and a full cast
2026 · Young Adults
Trans History: From Ancient Times to the Present Day — Alex L. Combs and Andrew Eakett · Produced by Listening Library · Narrated by the authors and a full cast

Announced January 26, 2026, at the ALA Youth Media Awards in Chicago. Honor titles included Jason Reynolds' Soundtrack and Whale Eyes.

January 18, 2026 — Voice Arts Awards

SOVAS · Beverly Hills

Run by the Society of Voice Arts and Sciences, with audiobook narration categories — the narrators' own awards, and the best window into which voices the industry itself rates. The 12th annual gala was held January 18, 2026, at the Beverly Hilton.

January 2026 — The lists

ALA lists in January · year-end lists each December

The ALA's Listen List picks the year's outstanding adult audiobooks, and Amazing Audiobooks for Young Adults does the same for younger listeners. Each December, the year-end best-of lists land — Audible, Libro.fm, Library Journal and Booklist — and this page gathers them in one place, with a price-check or free route for every title we can verify.
