AI in audiobooks · The catalogue

AI-narrated audiobooks — labelled, not hidden.

Every title on this page is narrated by a synthetic voice, and every listing says so, with the voice engine named. Our stance is the site's stance: we don't love or hate AI narration — we label it, listen to it, and tell you honestly when it's good, when it's flat, and when a human narrator is worth paying for. Use the switch above to filter the whole site; on this page it will empty the list, because AI is the whole list.

How these were picked

Free picks come from the Project Gutenberg Open Audiobook Collection (Project Gutenberg + Microsoft + MIT): public-domain classics read by Microsoft AI neural voices, free on Internet Archive. It's the strongest free AI narration we've found — and a zero-risk way to judge AI narration with your own ears.

Paid picks are Audible titles narrated with ElevenLabs or Amazon's Virtual Voice — the two engines behind most paid AI narration. We show the engine on every listing because quality varies by engine and by book; an AI credit should never be a surprise. Catalogue ported from our previous site and re-checked 2026-10-26; lengths marked “~” are as listed and approximate.
