Plans compared: listen all you want, three different ways.

Platform by platform

30-day free trial
Audible — $14.95/mo after a 30-day free trial

The world's largest audiobook catalog. Members get 1 credit per month to spend on any title, plus access to Audible Plus — a rotating library of thousands of included titles. You keep every book you buy, even if you cancel.

Catalog: 750,000+ titles · Platform figures checked October 2026

Unlimited access
Scribd — $11.99/mo

Unlimited streaming access to audiobooks, ebooks, sheet music, magazines, and documents — all for one flat monthly price. Best value for heavy readers who want variety; the catalog is curated but wide-ranging.

Catalog: 1M+ titles (audiobooks + ebooks) · Platform figures checked October 2026

Supports indie stores
Libro.fm — $14.99/mo

A catalogue of 400,000+ titles that overlaps Audible's — though Audible exclusives aren't included — and every purchase directly supports your chosen independent bookshop: your $14.99/mo credit goes to a local store instead of Amazon.

Catalog: 400,000+ titles · Platform figures checked October 2026

14-day free trial
Storytel — $14.99/mo after a 14-day free trial

Europe's leading audiobook platform, now available in the US and globally. Unlimited streaming with a strong international catalog and exclusive Storytel Originals — particularly good for non-English titles and Scandinavian literature.

Catalog: 500,000+ titles · Platform figures checked October 2026

DRM-free downloads
Downpour — $12.99/mo (club)

Own-your-audio platform from Blackstone Publishing. DRM-free MP3 downloads play on any device, forever. Club membership gives a monthly credit plus 30% off à-la-carte purchases — an Audible alternative for ownership purists.

Catalog: 100,000+ titles · Platform figures checked October 2026

No subscription needed
Google Play Books — Pay-per-title (no subscription)

No subscription required — buy individual audiobooks à la carte and they're yours forever, synced across all your Google-connected devices. Good for casual listeners who don't want a monthly commitment; frequent sales on popular titles.

Catalog: Large catalog · Platform figures checked October 2026

Apple devices only
Apple Books — Pay-per-title (no subscription)

Apple's built-in bookstore for iPhone and iPad users. Buy and own individual titles — no subscription needed — seamlessly integrated with iOS and macOS, with a strong catalog of bestsellers, frequent deals and curated staff picks.

Catalog: not published · Platform figures checked October 2026

15 hrs/month included with Premium
Spotify — Premium includes 15 hrs/month of audiobook listening; Audiobooks Access $9.99/mo (same 15 hrs, music stays on the free tier)

A third kind of plan: listening measured in hours, not credits. Included hours come with Premium (or the audiobooks-only Audiobooks Access plan); unused hours expire at month end and do not carry over, top-ups add 10 hours, and individual titles can also be bought à la carte via the Web Player. Market note: in Spotify's newest markets Premium includes 12 hrs/month from a 350k+ catalogue — hours are not the same everywhere.

Catalog: 700,000+ titles (Premium subscriber catalogue, US) · Figures checked Oct 22, 2026

The hours math (Spotify)

Spotify is the one plan priced in hours: Premium includes 15 hrs/month, and a book only costs the hours it actually runs — our price-check pages show each book's length against that allowance. A 30-hour epic takes two months of allowance (or a 10-hour top-up); a 7-hour novel fits inside one month with room to spare. Included hours expire monthly and never carry over, and the included hours are a Premium / Audiobooks Access benefit — not part of Spotify's free tier.
