Nov 5, 2026
Trade publishers are steering clear of AI narration

Trade publishers are largely not using AI voices for narration itself, while AI is already routine behind the scenes — editing, proofing and audio repair. The line the industry is drawing, for now, is between the voice in front of the listener and the tools behind it. (As reported by High Notes: The Art and Business of Voice.)

What changed for listeners
Nov 4, 2026
Spotify takes audiobooks to Pakistan — same 15 hours, a seventh of the US price

Spotify has switched audiobooks on in Pakistan: Audiobooks+ adds 15 hours a month for Rs 379 — about $1.37 at current rates. The same 15 hours sells as a $9.99 add-on in the US, and at RM17.50 — about $4.31 — in Malaysia: US $9.99 → Malaysia $4.31 → Pakistan $1.37 for the identical product. Medications are the famous case of one product priced to whatever each market will bear; digital listening now works the same way. Pakistan is one step in the expansion Spotify announced at the Frankfurt Book Fair (Oct 7): from 22 audiobook markets to more than 180 by the end of 2026, with Audiobooks+ already past one million subscribers and $100M in annual recurring revenue.
