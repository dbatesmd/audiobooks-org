Mon Oct 26, 2026
750+ stores count down to midnight for a print book — here's how to be listening at the same minute, free

More than 500 Barnes & Noble stores and ~250 Target countdowns mark the ACOTAR 6 print launch Monday night. The audiobook lands at the same 12:01am Tuesday moment — and the standard edition is free day-one on Hoopla, no waitlist. (Sources: Barnes & Noble Inc press release, Oct 19; AFP, Oct 23–24.)

Oct 7, 2026
Spotify takes audiobooks from 22 to 180+ markets

Spotify's global expansion makes it a real third kind of plan: hours-based. US Premium includes 15 hrs/month from a 700k+ catalogue; the new markets get 12 hrs/month and 350k+ titles, with Audiobooks+ the main route in much of Africa, Asia and Argentina. Our plan comparisons carry the market note — hours are not the same everywhere.

Oct 1, 2026
Audible adds AI inside the listening experience

Character Guide (live in limited beta on Dracula and 1984), Interactive Stories (rolling out this fall), and Visual Explorer (coming soon, debuting on The Cultural Tutor). Exoplanet adds Character Guide on Nov 5. None of this changes who narrates — our Human/Both/AI filter is unaffected.
